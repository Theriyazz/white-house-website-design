Why now 01 / 04

Families decide where to shop before they leave home

A family from Anand or Bharuch picks three or four shops the night before. Today, White House can only be found through Instagram, WhatsApp and word of mouth.

  • A

    Without a starting price, many parents assume a shop is expensive and skip it.

  • B

    Grooms search on Google first

    "Sherwani Vadodara" and "groom wear Raopura" go to whoever has a proper website.

  • C

    Timings, parking, price range and alteration time are answered one WhatsApp at a time.

  • D

    National chains look bigger

The wedding season runs November to February. A site that goes live now is working through the busiest months.
